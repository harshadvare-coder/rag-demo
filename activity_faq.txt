# id: faq_activities | category: activities

Q: How do I view available activities?
A: Open the application to see all available activities. Availability is shown in real time, so you only see activities that still have open slots.

Q: How do I choose an activity?
A: Browse the list of available activities and select your preferred activity before proceeding to booking.

Q: How do I book an activity?
A: Select an available activity, choose your preferred date and time, and proceed with the booking.

Q: Can I book an activity for someone else?
A: Yes, you can make a booking for another person by providing the required guest details during the booking process.

Q: Can I change the date or time of my activity?
A: Changes to the activity date or time depend on availability and the applicable booking policy. Check the booking details in the application for available options.

# id: faq_booking | category: booking

Q: How do I make a booking?
A: Select the activity you want, choose an available date and time, provide the required information, and confirm the booking.

Q: How do I know if my booking was successful?
A: After a successful booking, a confirmation will be displayed in the application. Your booking details will also be available in the application.

Q: Where can I find my booking details?
A: Open the application and go to your bookings to view your booking details, including the activity, date, time, and booking status.

Q: Can I make multiple bookings?
A: Yes, you can make multiple bookings as long as the selected activities have available slots.

Q: What happens if an activity is fully booked?
A: If an activity has no available slots, it will not be available for booking.

# id: faq_cancellation | category: cancellation

Q: Can I cancel my booking?
A: Bookings can be cancelled according to the applicable cancellation policy.

Q: How do I cancel my booking?
A: Open your booking details in the application and select the cancellation option if it is available.

Q: Will I receive a refund after cancelling my booking?
A: Refund eligibility depends on the applicable cancellation policy and the conditions associated with the booking.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refund processing time depends on the applicable refund policy and payment method.

Q: Can I cancel an activity after it has started?
A: Cancellation after the activity has started depends on the applicable cancellation policy.

# id: faq_availability | category: availability

Q: How can I check activity availability?
A: Open the activity section in the application to see activities that currently have available slots.

Q: Is activity availability updated in real time?
A: Yes, activity availability is shown in real time, so the displayed availability reflects the currently available slots.

Q: Why can't I see a particular activity?
A: An activity may not be displayed if it is unavailable, fully booked, or not currently offered.

# id: faq_payment | category: payment

Q: How do I pay for an activity?
A: Select an available activity, proceed through the booking process, and complete the payment using the payment method available in the application.

Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: The available payment methods are displayed during the payment step of the booking process.

Q: What should I do if my payment fails?
A: Check that your payment details are correct and try again. If the payment continues to fail, use another available payment method or contact support.

Q: Was I charged if my payment failed?
A: If you believe you were charged despite a failed payment, check your transaction history and contact support with the relevant booking or transaction details.

# id: faq_booking_confirmation | category: confirmation

Q: Where can I find my booking confirmation?
A: Your booking confirmation is available in the application after a successful booking.

Q: What information is included in a booking confirmation?
A: The booking confirmation includes the relevant booking details such as the activity, date, time, and booking status.

Q: What should I do if I do not receive a booking confirmation?
A: Check your bookings section in the application to verify whether the booking was created successfully. If the booking is not shown, contact support.

# id: faq_support | category: support

Q: How can I contact support?
A: Use the support or help section available in the application to contact customer support.

Q: What information should I provide when contacting support?
A: Provide relevant details such as your booking ID, activity name, booking date, and a description of the issue.

Q: What should I do if I have a problem with my booking?
A: Check your booking details first. If the issue cannot be resolved through the application, contact customer support with your booking information.

# id: faq_general | category: general

Q: What information can I get about an activity?
A: The application provides information about available activities and their relevant booking details.

Q: How do I find a specific activity?
A: Browse the available activities and use the available search or filtering options to find the activity you are looking for.

Q: Can I book an unavailable activity?
A: No. Only activities with available slots can be booked.

Q: Where can I see the status of my booking?
A: You can view the current status of your booking from the bookings section of the application.
